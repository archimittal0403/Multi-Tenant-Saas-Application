# 📖 Introduction

Educational institutions often rely on multiple disconnected systems to manage admissions, attendance, examinations, fee collection, student records, and academic operations. This fragmentation leads to duplicate data, manual work, inconsistent reporting, and increased administrative overhead.

**IRISERP** addresses these challenges by providing a unified, scalable, and secure Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) platform specifically designed for educational institutions.

Unlike conventional student management systems, IRISERP follows a **Multi-Tenant SaaS Architecture**, allowing multiple schools and colleges to operate independently within a single application while maintaining complete data isolation and security.

The system is built using a **metadata-driven architecture**, enabling dynamic customization of forms, modules, and workflows without modifying the application's core codebase. This design makes the platform highly flexible, maintainable, and suitable for institutions with different operational requirements.

Designed with scalability, modularity, and security in mind, IRISERP offers a modern solution capable of supporting institutions ranging from small schools to large universities.

The primary objective of IRISERP is to digitize and automate educational administration through a centralized platform that improves operational efficiency, enhances data security, and minimizes manual processes.

# 🏛 System Architecture

IRISERP follows a modular layered architecture consisting of Presentation Layer, Business Logic Layer, Data Access Layer, and Database Layer.

Each institution operates as an independent tenant while sharing the same application infrastructure. Tenant-specific information is maintained through isolated metadata and institute identifiers, ensuring complete logical separation of data.

The metadata-driven approach enables administrators to configure fields, modules, and workflows dynamically without changing application code, significantly reducing maintenance effort and improving extensibility.
